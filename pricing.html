Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden diagnosis or workshop bay fees?
None whatsoever. We provide a preliminary visual check free of charge. If in-depth teardown diagnostics are required, we quote the exact flat fee beforehand.
Why is motorcycle servicing priced differently than bicycle servicing?
Motorcycles operate under much higher thermal and kinematic loads, requiring pneumatic lifts, high-viscosity synthetic oils, valve feeler gauges, and hazardous fluid recycling compliance.
Do you offer discounts for college students or delivery couriers?
Yes! We offer 10% off workshop labor for students and commercial delivery riders upon showing a valid ID or delivery partner dashboard.
