Fitment Assurance

How to Identify Compatible Parts

Never install components that are approximate fits. Sub-millimeter discrepancies in chain pitch, axle spacing, or caliper mounts create severe mechanical hazards. Follow our 4-step identification process:

1

Check Vehicle Manufacturer

Locate manufacturer plate on headtube or steering stem.

2

Identify Exact Model and Production Year

Braking standards and chain pitches frequently revise between model years.

3

Verify Component Stamp & Specifications

Read tyre ETRTO markings (e.g. 37-622) or chain link model stamps.

4

Confirm Free Compatibility with Workshop

Send us a photo on WhatsApp or submit our form below for mechanic verification.

Check Part Availability
