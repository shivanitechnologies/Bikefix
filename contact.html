Customer Protocol

Vehicle Drop-Off Instructions

We make bringing your vehicle in painless, transparent, and completely documented:

1

Arrive During Working Hours

Pull into our reception driveway anytime between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

2

Meet Your Assigned Service Technician

A certified mechanic greets you at the bay to understand your riding symptoms firsthand.

3

Explain Symptoms & Riding History

Tell us about recent squeaks, gear slip, oil consumption, or upcoming tours planned.

4

Complete Complimentary 15-Min Initial Inspection

We inspect brake pads, chain tension, tyre wear, and cables together on the hydraulic lift.

5

Receive Binding Digital Repair Estimate

You receive a written estimate link via SMS/WhatsApp before leaving the workshop. No hidden charges.

Before You Visit Our Workshop

Documents to Carry

For motorcycles, please bring a copy of vehicle RC and insurance (mandatory for road test rides). For bicycles, no registration paperwork is required.

Drop-Off Timing Tip

For same-day collection on full overhaul packages, we recommend dropping your bike off between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM to allow thorough cooling and testing.

Customer Lounge

While waiting for express 30-minute puncture fixes or chain lubes, relax in our air-conditioned lounge with high-speed Wi-Fi, espresso, and workshop viewing windows.

Courtesy Loaner Bikes
