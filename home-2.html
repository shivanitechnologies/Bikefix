Rider Education

Mechanical Care Explained

Understand the physics of your machinery. Click each component to learn when maintenance is critical.

Topic 01

Why Chain Maintenance Matters

Chains do not physically stretch; rather, their internal pins and bushings wear down, increasing the pitch distance between links. A worn chain cuts sharp 'shark-fin' valleys into your cassette and chainrings, eventually causing chain skip under load.

Workshop Threshold: Replace bicycle chains at 0.5% elongation to preserve expensive sprockets. On motorcycles, check sag tension every 800 km.
Topic 02

When Brakes Need Immediate Inspection

Hydraulic brake levers that pull squishy against the handlebar indicate moisture contamination or vapor bubbles in brake fluid. Metal-on-metal squealing means pad friction material is below the 1.5mm safety backing plate limit.

Workshop Threshold: Full hydraulic fluid flush every 12 months; disc pads replaced when thickness drops under 1.5mm.
Topic 03

Signs of Critical Tyre Wear

Micro-cracks along the tyre sidewall indicate ozone dry rot. Flat-spotting in the center reduces cornering grip, while embedded flint shards lead to high-speed blowouts.

Workshop Threshold: Inspect tread depth indicators and check air pressure weekly using a calibrated digital manometer.
Topic 04

Why Regular Servicing Prevents Costly Failures

Preventive bearing repacks, cable housing lubrications, and spoke tension balances cost a fraction of replacing stripped freehubs, blown head gaskets, or cracked alloy rims.

Workshop Threshold: Complete overhaul every 5,000 km or annually, whichever comes first.
