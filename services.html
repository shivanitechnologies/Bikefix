Repair Service Details & Specifications

Everything you need to know before booking: what is included, time requirements, and quality standards.

What is included in a Full General Servicing?
A 32-point bumper-to-bumper procedure: Complete ultrasonic degreasing of cassette and chain; brake calipers centered and bled; headset and bottom bracket re-greased; wheel spokes tension checked; gear derailleur indexed; cables lubricated; all bolts torqued to manufacturer Newton-meter specs; and a road test by our head mechanic.
Who needs this service and how frequently?
Every daily commuter should schedule an essential check every 4 to 6 months. For endurance cyclists and highway motorcyclists, a complete care overhaul is recommended prior to any major multi-day ride or at 3,000 km intervals.
What is the typical inspection and turnaround time?
Preliminary inspection takes 15–20 minutes. Minor repairs (punctures, brake pad swap, cable replacement) are completed within 45–60 minutes. Full general overhauls typically require same-day pickup (drop off by 10 AM, collect by 6 PM).
How are estimated charges calculated?
Our rates are published and fixed. Labor charges are standardized by repair item. If parts replacement (chain, pads, inner tubes) is required, we provide written options (Standard OEM vs Premium Aftermarket) with itemized retail pricing before installation.
Important customer information regarding warranties
All workshop labor is backed by our 30-Day BikeFix Workmanship Guarantee. If gear shifting goes out of alignment or brake rub occurs within 30 days of service, bring your bike back for a free micro-tune adjustment with no questions asked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered to make your workshop visit effortless.

Do I need to book an appointment or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are always welcome for emergency punctures, tube swaps, and quick cable fixes. For full general servicing, tune-ups, or motorcycle engine diagnostics, booking an online appointment reserves your dedicated mechanic bay and guarantees faster turnaround.
Do you service electric bicycles and e-scooters?
Yes! We provide mechanical maintenance for e-bikes including hydraulic disc brakes, heavy-duty e-bike rated chains, wheel truing, and puncture repairs. (Note: we do not service proprietary internal high-voltage battery electronics).
Can I bring my own replacement spare parts?
Yes. You are welcome to supply your own compatible components. We will inspect them for safety and fitment, and only charge our standard labor installation fee.
What brands of motorcycles do you service?
We service all major brands: Royal Enfield, KTM, Honda, Yamaha, Bajaj, TVS, Suzuki, Hero, Kawasaki, and BMW Motorrad.
What happens if unexpected damage is found during repair?
We take photos of the defective part and send them immediately via WhatsApp or phone with an explanation and cost estimate. We never proceed with unapproved paid work.
What payment methods do you accept at the workshop?
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash. Digital tax invoices are emailed immediately upon settlement.
